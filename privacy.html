Privacy Policy — CalcPro

Effective date: 2026-07-23
Last updated: 2026-07-23

1. Who we are

CalcPro ("we", "our", "the app") is a mobile calculator application published by PowerX Solutions. Contact: info@powerxsolutions.com.

2. Information we collect

The CalcPro app itself does not ask you to create an account, does not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, files, or location. Everything you type into the calculator stays on your device.

However, CalcPro displays advertisements from third-party ad networks (Google AdMob). To serve ads, these networks may collect:

  • Your device's Advertising ID (a resettable identifier under your control in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Approximate IP-address-based location (country / region level, not GPS).
  • Basic device information: model, operating system version, language, screen size.
  • Interaction with ads (impressions, clicks) for fraud prevention and billing.

We do not receive this data ourselves in personally identifiable form. Google's own privacy policy governs it: https://policies.google.com/privacy

3. Firebase

The app fetches ad configuration values from Firebase Remote Config (a Google service). Firebase logs an anonymous installation ID for the sole purpose of delivering config values. No personal data is transmitted.

4. Children

CalcPro is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the app in a way that collected data, contact info@powerxsolutions.com and we will delete any associated records.

5. Your controls

  • To reset or opt out of personalized advertising: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of ads personalization".
  • To request data associated with your Advertising ID be deleted, contact Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/7666366

6. Data retention

Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, we retain none. Ad-network data retention is governed by the respective network's policy.

7. Third-party services used

  • Google AdMob (advertising)
  • Google Firebase Remote Config (configuration only)
  • Google Play Services (required by the Android operating system)

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect changes. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the new policy.

9. Contact

Questions or requests: info@powerxsolutions.com

10. Governing law

This policy is interpreted under the laws of India. Disputes will be subject to the courts of Surat, Gujarat.
